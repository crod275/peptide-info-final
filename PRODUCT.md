# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, biohackers, and health-curious individuals looking up structured information on peptides — dosage protocols, benefits, mechanism of action, side effects, and research citations. They use this site as a reference layer, not a shopping destination. Context: typically focused, looking up a specific peptide or comparing a few, reading on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

PeptideRef is a structured research reference for peptide information. It exists to surface accurate, well-organized information — dosage, benefits, research, side effects — in a format that respects the reader's intelligence. Success looks like a user finding exactly what they need quickly and trusting what they read.

## Brand Personality

Scientific, trustworthy, calm. Reference-grade authority without clinical coldness. Closer to Stripe Docs or Vercel Docs than a supplement store.

## Anti-references

- Neon biohacker aesthetics (bright greens, electric blues on dark)
- Crypto/Web3 visual language
- Supplement sales pages (aggressive CTAs, before/after imagery, hype copy)
- Flashy SaaS startup templates (gradient text, hero-metric dashboards, glassmorphism as default)
- Generic "wellness" sites (soft pastels, curved everything, feel-good stock photos)

## Design Principles

1. **Research-grade clarity** — every element serves information legibility. Decoration earns its place.
2. **Trust through restraint** — clean, precise, unhurried. Does not shout. Does not sell.
3. **Expert defaults** — sensible information hierarchy so readers can skim to depth, not wade through it.
4. **References as citizens** — citations and source links are first-class UI, not footnote afterthoughts.
5. **Calm motion** — animation is purposeful and subtle. Nothing pulses, bounces, or loops.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum across all text and interactive elements. Full keyboard navigation. Reduced-motion support via `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`. Mobile-comfortable tap targets (44×44px minimum).
